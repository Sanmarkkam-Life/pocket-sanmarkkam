🪔 *Pocket Sanmarkkam is here (beta)*

Vallalar's words, now in your pocket. A free app with the Thiru Arutpa and his prose writings: translation, the original Tamil, transliteration and commentary.

📖 17 books, readable offline
🌅 A verse every day, ready to share
🔥 Ask KarunAI about any verse
🎶 The Maha Mantra, the Agaval and audiobooks

👉 https://pocket-sanmarkkam.vercel.app

It is a *beta*, and we need your help. Use it for a few days, then tap *Feedback* (bottom right) and tell us:
• what helped you
• what was hard or confusing
• any mistake you find in a book

📲 *Install it on your phone:*
Android: open the link in Chrome → ⋮ menu → *Add to Home screen*
iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share button → *Add to Home Screen*

Free, no account, no ads. Please share it with anyone who would love it. 🙏
